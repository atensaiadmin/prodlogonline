# Concept

## Problem

A founder has 10–20 ideas in flight at any time. They capture them in notes, markdown files, or a Notion doc. But there is no structured place to:

- See all ideas at a glance with their current stage
- Log progress against an idea over time
- Record why an idea stalled or what unblocked it
- Know, months later, whether an idea is alive or dead

The result: ideas languish. Founders lose context when they revisit an idea weeks later. Execution momentum fragments across tools.

## Persona

- Solo founder or very early-stage team (1–3 people)
- Pre-product-market-fit
- Has multiple ideas, some active, some dormant
- Uses notes/docs extensively but wants a single pane of glass
- Values speed and simplicity over features

## Core loop

1. Founder has an idea → adds it to prodlog (takes 10 seconds)
2. Founder works on the idea → logs an entry (note the date, what happened, how they feel about it)
3. Founder advances the stage or marks it dead
4. Over time, prodlog becomes a timeline of every idea they pursued and why

## Design principles

- **Frictionless capture** — adding an idea should take less than 15 seconds
- **Beautiful but minimal** — pleasant to look at, nothing unnecessary
- **Log over status** — the progress log (timeline of entries) is the heart of the app, not a kanban board
- **Local-first optional** — should work well as a single-user app, multi-user is a stretch goal
